Frequently Asked Questions

Answers about shipping, returns, and orders.

What is your return policy?

We accept returns within 30 days of purchase for unworn items with original tags attached. Items must be in their original condition. Refunds will be processed to your original payment method within 5-7 business days after we receive your return.

How long does shipping take?

Standard shipping typically takes 5-7 business days. Express shipping (2-3 business days) is available at checkout for an additional fee. You will receive a tracking number via email once your order ships.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes, we ship to select countries worldwide. International shipping times vary by location but typically take 10-14 business days. Additional customs fees and duties may apply depending on your country's regulations.

How do I track my order?

Once your order ships, you'll receive an email with a tracking number. You can also view your order status by logging into your account and visiting the Orders page. Tracking information may take 24-48 hours to update after shipping.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express, Discover), PayPal, Apple Pay, and Google Pay. All transactions are securely processed and encrypted for your safety.

How do I find my size?

Each product page includes a detailed size guide. We recommend measuring yourself and comparing to our size chart. If you're between sizes, we suggest sizing up for a more relaxed fit. Contact our customer service team if you need personalized sizing assistance.

Can I change or cancel my order?

Orders can be modified or cancelled within 1 hour of placement. After this time, orders are processed for shipping and cannot be changed. Please contact customer service immediately if you need to make changes to a recent order.

Do you offer gift wrapping?

Yes, we offer complimentary gift wrapping for all orders. Simply select the gift wrapping option at checkout and include a personalized message. Your order will arrive in our signature F17 gift packaging.

How can I contact customer support?

Our customer support team is available Monday-Friday, 9am-6pm EST. You can reach us via email at support@f17.com or call us at 1-800-F17-SHOP. We typically respond to emails within 24 hours.

Are your products sustainable?

Sustainability is at the core of F17. We use organic and recycled materials whenever possible, partner with ethical manufacturers, and minimize waste in our production process. Learn more about our sustainability practices on our About page.
